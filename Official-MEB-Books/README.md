# Official MEB Grade 6 English Books (2026–2027 · TYMM)

This directory contains the unmodified official **6th Grade English** books and listening transcripts published by the **Republic of Türkiye Ministry of National Education (T.C. Millî Eğitim Bakanlığı — MEB)** under the *Türkiye Yüzyılı Maarif Modeli (TYMM)* curriculum (`30.09.2026` edition).
